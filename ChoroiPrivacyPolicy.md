Choroi Privacy Policy
Last updated: 18 November 2026

Choroi is a Japanese-learning app made by Supah Software. This policy explains what Choroi collects, why, who helps us handle it, and how to delete it. We don't sell your personal information.

**Your account**

Choroi needs an account so your progress is never lost. You sign in with Google or with an email address and password, handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We keep your email address, your name (from Google, or the one you enter) and an account ID that Firebase creates.

**Your progress and backups**

Your lesson and quest progress, review schedule, practice scores, daily goals, streaks, trophies and settings are stored on your device. While you're signed in, Choroi also backs them up to Google Cloud Firestore, tied to your account, so you can restore them on a new phone. Only you can read your backup, and we never use your progress for advertising.

If you have Android's own backup turned on, Android may also include Choroi's data in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is managed by Google and you, and you can turn it off in your device's settings.

**Usage stats and crash reports**

Choroi uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics to learn how the app gets used (for example, where lessons get skipped) and to fix crashes. These include app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics such as device model and app version, and an app installation ID. They never include your name, email address or answers, and Analytics does not read your advertising ID. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, nothing is sent until you agree. Anywhere, you can turn it off in Settings > Privacy > Share usage stats.

**Ads (Freemium only)**

Choroi only shows ads if you choose Freemium, which unlocks Premium in exchange for ads. If you do, Google AdMob shows the ads and may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, device information and how you interact with ads. It uses these to show, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks you first, and if you say no you'll only see non-personalised ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings at any time. If you never choose Freemium, the ads SDK never starts. See [how Google uses data from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

**Purchases**

Subscriptions are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Each purchase carries a scrambled (hashed) form of your account ID so Premium stays with your Choroi account.

**Handwriting practice**

Writing practice uses Google ML Kit's Digital Ink Recognition. The first time you use it, the app downloads Google's Japanese handwriting model. Recognition then runs entirely on your device, and what you write is never sent to us or to Google. ML Kit may send Google basic diagnostic information, such as device model, operating system version, app version and performance figures. It does not include anything you write. See [ML Kit's data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure).

**Downloads from Google Play**

Choroi and its optional content, such as audio packs, are downloaded through Google Play under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The audio was recorded or generated ahead of time: nothing you say or type is ever sent to make it.

**Who handles your data**

Google provides every service above (Firebase, AdMob, Google Play and ML Kit) under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Data is encrypted in transit. We don't share your information with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

**Deleting your account**

In Choroi, go to Settings > Account > Delete account. This deletes your account and your cloud backup straight away. Uninstalling the app then removes what's left on your phone. You can also email supahsoftware@gmail.com from the address on your account and we'll delete your account and backup within 30 days. Usage stats and crash reports aren't linked to your account and expire on their own (Analytics data after at most 14 months, crash reports after 90 days).

**Children**

Choroi isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child under 13 has made an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

**Changes**

If what Choroi collects changes, this policy will be updated before that change ships, and the date above will change.

Questions? Contact supahsoftware@gmail.com.
