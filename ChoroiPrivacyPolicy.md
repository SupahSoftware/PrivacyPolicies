Choroi Privacy Policy
Last updated: 12 November 2026

Choroi is a Japanese-learning app made by Supah Software. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking. We don't collect, store or sell any personal information.

**What stays on your device**

Everything you do in Choroi is stored only on your device: your lesson and quest progress, review schedule, practice scores, daily goal, streak and settings. None of it is sent to us. Uninstalling the app removes it.

**Handwriting practice**

Writing practice uses Google ML Kit's Digital Ink Recognition. The first time you use it, the app downloads Google's Japanese handwriting model. Recognition then runs entirely on your device, and what you write is never sent to us or to Google. Like other ML Kit features, it may send Google basic diagnostic information, such as device model, operating system version, app version and performance figures, to keep the feature working. It does not include anything you write or any information that identifies you. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [ML Kit's data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure).

**Downloads from Google Play**

Choroi is installed and updated through Google Play, and optional content such as audio packs is downloaded through Google Play as well. These downloads are handled by Google under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). If you've agreed to share usage and diagnostics with Google on your device, Google Play may also give us anonymous crash and performance reports, which don't identify you. The audio itself was recorded or generated ahead of time; nothing you say or type is ever sent to make it.

**Backups**

If you have Android's backup turned on, Android may include Choroi's progress in your device backup to your Google account, so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is managed by Google and you, never by us. You can turn it off in your device's settings.

**Children**

Choroi doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child's information has somehow reached us, contact us and we'll delete it.

**Changes**

If Choroi ever starts collecting information, for example to support a subscription, this policy will be updated before that change ships, and the date above will change.
