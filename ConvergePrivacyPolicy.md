Converge Privacy Policy
Last updated: 11 July 2026

Converge does not collect, store, or share any personal information. All data you create including your movie rankings and app settings are stored only on your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

Converge loads movie poster images from The Movie Database (TMDB) content delivery network. These are standard image requests and do not include any personal or identifying information about you.

Converge contains no ads, no analytics, and no third-party tracking. If you back up your rankings, the backup file is written to a location you choose and remains under your control.

Because no data is collected, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

Questions? Contact supahsoftware@gmail.com.
